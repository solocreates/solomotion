# The Playground prompt book

Every scene in this project was built by Claude Opus from one prompt. This is the
whole book: the prompts that made the scenes (★) and the ones still waiting to
be made. Paste any of them to Claude with the studio's MCP linked. Change one
word and you get a different film; that's the point.

How a good prompt is shaped: **subject · style · palette · motion · sound · length/format**.
Name a feeling, not a feature ("calm", "smug", "late at night"). Ask for one hero
per frame. Say what must NOT happen ("no drop shadows", "no voice-over").

## A. Type that moves
1. ★ **Swiss Grid** — "A 1080×1350 International Typographic Style poster that assembles itself: cream paper, signal red, black Inter Display. A 12-column grid draws on as hairlines, then the words FORM FOLLOWS MOTION slide into their columns one by one on cubic-bezier(.76,0,.24,1). A giant red '01' rotates 90° into place last. 6 s, loops."
2. ★ **Font Roulette** — "One word, 'yes', cycles through twelve wildly different fonts like a slot machine: fast at first, slowing, landing on a heavy italic serif in clay. Hard cuts only, no fades. Each landing ticks. 5 s, square."
3. **Pull Quote** — "An editorial pull quote on warm oat paper: Instrument Serif italic, word by word, a hand-drawn clay marker sweep under the key phrase, the author's byline in tiny wide caps. Magazine calm. 6 s."
4. "Kinetic lyric: 4 lines of a song, each word lands on the beat, the stressed word twice the size. Black ground, one acid-green accent."
5. "A countdown from 10 to 1 where every number is a different art style: pixel, neon, chrome, chalk, paper-cut, riso… lands on 'GO' in the brand colour."
6. "Variable-weight breathing word: 'breathe' swells from Thin to Black and back on a 4 s cycle, letter spacing opening as it inhales. Fog-blue on ink."
7. "Text on a path: a sentence rides a wavy hand-drawn line across the screen like a ribbon in wind."
8. "A typographic 'manifesto': six short sentences, each with its own type treatment and colour block, cut on a 120 BPM grid."

## B. Pixel and game worlds

## Remix moves (apply to ANY scene)
- "Same scene, but in pixel art" · "…as a risograph print" · "…in neon at night" · "…on paper with line boil".
- "Make it calmer: half the moves, double the hold."
- "Make it loop seamlessly."
- "Add sound: one real sound per thing that has weight, on its frame."
- "Turn it into a 9:16 reel with a hook in the first second."
- "Give it a character who reacts."
